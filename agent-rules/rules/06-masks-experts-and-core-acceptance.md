# MASKS, multidisciplinary corrections, and acceptance

Core acceptance checks every final question/solution pair, complete positions, independent concern coverage, source support, cross-field agreement, solution dependencies and all language counterparts. Structural validity is not semantic correctness. Keep exact reviewed digests and reasoned decisions. Human production supervision may be none/selective/full; record actual interventions separately from configured policy.

Expert corrections include reviewer identity and competence scope, source/evidence, parent artifact, proposed change, rationale, decision, accepted target and affected dependencies. Route legal interpretation, technical evidence, implementation, formal wording and language equivalence to qualified scopes. Do not invent people or treat one discipline's approval as global authority. Preserve disagreement and initial opinions. Accepted edits feed later bases and source-linked regression fixtures; unaccepted edits remain candidates.

For requested legal masks, decompose provisions into assessable conditions and retain instrument/version/article, jurisdiction, entity/actor, effective dates/precision, scope, applicability assumptions, selected SFs, evidence, rationale, references, partial/uncovered obligations and review status. Source discovery on NIS2/ENISA can inform masks; it cannot itself establish coverage or national applicability.

Create or rebind exactly one Access selection form: complete catalog fields, SFS selector, or nonempty distinct SF-pair list. Validate four-digit fields, leading zeros, indices, expansion, bit order and target-language catalog digest. Zero mask bits exclude positions, not require negative service outcomes. An empty library differs from an all-zero mask. Pending selected answers prevent a completed operational check.

Agent-evaluated legal masks start as drafts. Operational `reviewed` status requires a published catalog edition, named maintainer, accepted review, named reviewer(s) and timestamp under the actual schema. Model acceptance cannot manufacture those records. Incompatible/missing prior definitions leave mappings unresolved outside the accepted compatible library; a required unbindable mask makes that requested package incomplete.

Use independently typed catalog-quality vectors for MASK-selected analysis. Never present a source-support or clarity indicator as a system's compliance answer. Report criterion, selected/resolved/pending counts, denominators, semantic mapping and uncertainty separately. Counts are not risk probabilities or compliance certificates.

Greek NIS2 outputs cite the applicable official laws, amendments and implementing decisions at cutoff, including Law 5160/2024 where applicable. No model is an authoritative legal interpreter. Missing support, unclear entity scope or contradictory sources block the relevant claim.

Stop bounded repairs on exhaustion, repeated candidate digests or unresolved support. Repair the earliest affected stage and repeat applicable checks, including previously passing dependent fields. Acceptance is a record of executed checks on immutable inputs; an inserted status string is not a review.
