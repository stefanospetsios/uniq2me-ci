# DORA application of the UNIQ2Me methodology

The unchanged main.tex preserves the UNIQ2Me research methodology. Its original
cybersecurity/NIS2 demonstration topics are not this controller's topic allocation.
profiles/dora.json supplies the trusted DORA allocation. These sixteen areas are
an authored analytical taxonomy, not sixteen official DORA categories or a claim
that the regulation specifies 256 questions.

Retain sixteen SFS positions 0–F, sixteen SF positions 0–F within each set, the
existing record schema and 512-character question limit. SF 0 is the most
significant bit; SF F is the least significant bit. A set is sixteen bits encoded
as four hexadecimal digits; a complete profile has sixteen fields. Only evidenced,
reviewed binary assessments contribute bits. Missing evidence stays pending.
Authoring questions, solutions, translations or MASKs is not an operational
assessment, legal-compliance determination or human approval.

Each question defines one bounded condition, actor/entity role, system/function
scope, evidence period, positive/negative/missing rule and alternatives. Record
proportionality, exemptions, standard/simplified regime, TLPT selection and
critical-provider designation. Never assign supervisory powers to financial
entities or designated-provider duties to every ICT vendor. Non-applicability is
handled by explicit scope selection, never a fabricated pass or failure. No
DORA applicability MASK is generated implicitly.

Questions precede proposed solutions and explanations/translations. Retain
structural, fixture, evidence, same-model review, lineage and translation checks.
Do not invent signatures, scores, identities, execution results or training.
Human review and assessment evidence remain separate artifacts.

Each live topic needs full primary Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 plus relevant official
support, observed search URLs and checked quotations. Research applicable RTS/ITS,
versions, corrigenda, amendments and authority requirements at the run cutoff.
Discovery seeds and article mappings are not admitted evidence. Keep laws typed
law, guidance typed guidance and implementation documentation typed technical-doc.
NIS2 and Greek Law 5160/2024 are not mandatory DORA instruments.

Use json/<language>/<SFS>.json and repo-sf/<language>/<repoId>.json unchanged.
Root catalog-profile.json, taxonomy digest and the ordinary catalog digest bind
each catalog. DORA positions differ in meaning from generic cybersecurity
positions: never mix assessments or reuse generic MASKs. A changed taxonomy
requires an explicit new version and compatibility review.

Research lives in uniq2me-rag/main. Catalog output lives in a separate DORA
instance in uniq2me-ai, normally en/dora-qwen3. Keep the owner's .github code
and unrelated branches intact. Local preparation authorizes no remote push.
